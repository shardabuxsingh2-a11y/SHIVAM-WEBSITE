OUR STORY

A fan-first sports media initiative.

Shivam Patel Sports Media is a sports information and digital community initiative based in Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, India. We aim to share accessible sports updates, explainers, features and digital coverage for people who enjoy following sport.

Our work may include publishing sports-related information, operating digital media properties, creating editorial and social content, and supporting respectful online conversations among fans.

We believe our audience deserves clear communication. Business registration details, contact information and service descriptions on this website should always reflect verified, current information.
